## 1. What PayLock does

PayLock lets:
1. Sellers list digital / physical products
2. Buyers fund a wallet
3. Buyer buys — funds move to **Held** escrow
4. Seller delivers
5. Buyer confirms delivery — funds are **Released** to seller
6. Seller withdraws to a verified bank account

PayLock is a marketplace and escrow facilitator. The seller is responsible for the product and delivery. The buyer is responsible for checking the product and confirming only after delivery.

- Auto-release does not guarantee the product was correct. Always check before the 48-hour window ends. If there is a problem, open a dispute before auto-release.
- PayLock may extend, hold, or reverse escrow in cases of fraud, dispute, or violation of these Terms.
